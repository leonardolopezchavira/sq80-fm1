Playing

K1–K4CUTOFFRESONATTACKRELEASE

The program's own filter, and ENV 4's attack (T1) and release (T4). A CYAN name has unsaved changes. The last note played shows top right.

HOME: scope · RANDOM

HOME on the playing screen: the scope (the waveform, large), again: RANDOM, again: back.

RANDOMAMOUNTPARTSNEWSTORED

AMOUNT 5–100 % (how far values move) · PARTS: ALL, OSC, FILTER, ENV, LFO, MOD · NEW: right a new program, left the one before (8 kept) · STORED: back to the program as stored. Named RND001…; keep one with SAVE.

Saving

SAVE from any screen · SELECT or K1: the bank · PRESETS or K2: the place (the screen shows what you would replace) · SAVE again stores · HOME cancels.

The bank, program, octave, effects, arpeggiator and sequencer remember themselves a few seconds after a change.

Arpeggiator

ARP tap: on / off · hold the keys to arpeggiate · hold ARP: its pages.

Sequencer: up to 64 steps

  • SEQ: STEP entry: play a note or chord (up to 4) into the marked step; let go, the mark moves on. K3: NOTE, TIE, REST · K4: accent, slide.
  • Hold SEQ (or tap it again): the 16 white keys are steps 1–16; the first 4 black keys (or OCT−/+) choose the page of 16. Press an empty step: the last note · press a full one and let go: clear · hold it and turn K1–K4.
  • PLAY start / stop (it flashes on the beat) · REC while playing: record, layer on layer · REC stopped: armed, your first note starts.
  • Hold REC 2 s: clear · EDIT+OCT− undo · EDIT+OCT+ redo.

GLO

CPU now and PEAK, XRUN (should stay 0), voices, cores · GLO again: peak to 0 · PLAY here: all 8 voices, a test.

GLOCLICKMIN VEL

CLICK: OFF, REC, ON · MIN VEL: the softest the keys play (0–127).

MIDI · the web pages

In over USB, the MIDI jack and Bluetooth, any channel: notes, velocity, bend, aftertouch, mod wheel, sustain, program changes, clock. The keys send over USB and Bluetooth.
