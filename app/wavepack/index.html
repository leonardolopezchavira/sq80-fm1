SQ-80 wave pack maker

Your own sounds as SQ-80 waves: WAV files (or AIFF, MP3, FLAC: what this browser reads) into a .rompack that the FM-1, the editor and the emulator play like any other. Everything happens in this browser: nothing is uploaded. Use sounds you have the right to share if you will share the pack: your own, public domain / CC0, or licensed for it.

Slots 0–53 loop (a cycle of the sound: give it a steady, pitched sound); 54–74 play once (drums, plucks, attacks). Drop a file on a row to put it there.
How the sounds are made into waves

Looped slots: AUTO finds the sound's pitch in its steady part and takes one cycle; WHOLE takes the whole file as one cycle (a single-cycle file); FRAME takes one frame of a wavetable file (2048 samples a frame, e.g. from Serum or Vital). The cycle becomes four tables, one for each key range, with only the harmonics that range can play cleanly, as the original waves are. One-shot slots: the sound from its start (silence trimmed), at 24 kHz (lower when it is long, to fit 32,768 samples), tuned to play at its own speed on its ROOT key. All are 8-bit, as the SQ-80's are.
