SQ-80 loader

Puts your SQ-80 waves and programs on an FM-1 running SQ-80. Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.

1 · Connect

Close other pages and apps that use the FM-1's MIDI first.

2 · ROM pack

Your .rompack file, made from your own SQ-80 ROM dumps by tools/make_rompack.py. It holds all 75 waves (about 264 KB) and takes a minute or so. The FM-1 is silent while it loads.


  


  

    
3 · Programs

    
An ESQ-1 or SQ-80 SysEx file (.syx): a 40-program bank, or single programs (the first 40 are
      taken). It goes into one of the FM-1's eight banks, replacing what was there.
