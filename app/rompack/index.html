SQ-80 ROM pack maker

For owners of an Ensoniq SQ-80 and its ROM dumps: your dumps become sq80.rompack, the original waves for SQ-80 on the FM-1. Everything happens in this browser: the files are not uploaded anywhere.

1 · Your ROM dumps

Six files from your SQ-80:

· the four wave ROMs, 64 KB each, often named 2202, 2203, 2204, 2205 (any extension, e.g. .bin) — or one 256 KB image of all four;
· the OS ROM, its two halves of 32 KB each (low and high, often .low / .hig), or one 64 KB file. It holds the list of the 75 waves: their names and which part of the wave ROMs each key plays.

Drop them here, or click to choose (all at once)

An ESQ-1's ROMs are not enough: it has two wave ROMs and its own OS, without the SQ-80's 43 added waves.
